# Orgtree 3.1.0

- **Reshaping the org is fast again.** Moving an agent, hiring above or
  beside one, swapping seats, promoting, demoting and the other ways of
  reshaping the org no longer slow down with the org's archived history.
  On a large org, moving a manager with many retired reports went from
  about 40 seconds to under a second. The first start after the update
  makes a small change to the database to support this.
- **The org inbox stays clear in the circle view.** When a ring of agents
  grows over the org inbox card, the card slides outward just far enough to
  stay clear of agent cards, their credit bars and document chips, and the
  open hire draft. It moves back when they go. The row view is unchanged.
- **Right-click menu on the empty canvas.** Right-clicking the canvas
  background offers Fit the whole org, Jump to the switchboard, and Focus
  last desk, which returns to the agent desk you used most recently,
  including one in a pinned window.
